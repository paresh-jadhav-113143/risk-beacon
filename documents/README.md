# Supplier Risk Intelligence Platform

This folder contains architecture artifacts for an agentic AI application that evaluates organizational and supplier risk during pre-onboarding and after onboarding.

The design is based on the `Semicolon_Apex.docx` concept, especially these product areas:

1. Risk Data Enrichment
2. Dynamic Risk Management
3. Authenticity Detection
4. Anomaly Detection
5. Risk Analytics Over Time
