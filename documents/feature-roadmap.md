# Feature Roadmap

## MVP

Focus on pre-onboarding risk assessment and human decisioning.

- Supplier onboarding form
- Supplier profile management
- Document upload
- OCR and document extraction
- Basic risk enrichment from selected sources
- Supplier identity resolution
- Sanctions/watchlist screening
- Category-wise risk scoring
- Composite risk score
- AI-generated evidence summary
- Human approval workflow
- Approve, reject, defer, and request information actions
- Audit trail
- In-app and email notifications

## Phase 2: Continuous Monitoring

Move from static onboarding assessment to dynamic risk management.

- Continuous monitoring for onboarded suppliers
- Event-driven enrichment refresh
- Risk score recalculation
- Score movement explanation
- Configurable thresholds
- Risk owner assignment
- Investigation case management
- Supplier clarification workflow
- Teams/Slack/webhook notifications
- Risk trend timeline

## Phase 3: Trust, Authenticity, and Anomaly Intelligence

Strengthen fraud and hidden-risk detection.

- Document forgery detection
- Certificate authenticity verification
- Metadata inconsistency detection
- Cross-source verification
- Signature, stamp, logo, and template anomaly checks
- Supplier behavior anomaly detection
- Peer group comparison
- Unusual ownership, contact, document, payment, and shipment pattern detection

## Phase 4: Predictive Risk Analytics

Identify future risk candidates before failure occurs.

- Temporal risk trajectories
- 3, 6, and 12 month risk forecasts
- Supplier deterioration prediction
- Similarity analysis against previously failed suppliers
- Cohort analysis by geography, industry, tier, commodity, and business unit
- What-if simulation
- Preventive audit recommendations
- Automated mitigation recommendations

## Phase 5: Enterprise Integrations and Scale

Operationalize the platform across procurement, compliance, and supplier management.

- SAP Ariba integration
- Coupa integration
- Oracle Procurement integration
- Microsoft Dynamics integration
- GRC integration
- ServiceNow/Jira case integration
- SSO and enterprise RBAC
- Advanced audit exports
- Policy-as-code scoring configuration
- Multi-region deployment
- Data retention and privacy controls

## Suggested Delivery Sequence

1. Build supplier intake, document upload, and basic risk scoring.
2. Add human review workflow and audit trail.
3. Add enrichment connectors for sanctions, news, legal/regulatory, and financial sources.
4. Add continuous monitoring and event-driven score recalculation.
5. Add authenticity and anomaly detection.
6. Add predictive analytics and enterprise integrations.
